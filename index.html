A few quick answers

FAQs.

Some useful details before we start working together.

What kind of projects do you take?

Social media posts, stories, carousels, café promotions, menus, posters and basic brand identity work.

How do I get a quote?

Send your project type, quantity, deadline and references. I'll reply to discuss the scope and pricing.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. The number of revisions and final deliverables will be agreed before the project starts.

Do you work outside Dehradun?

Yes. Projects can be discussed and delivered remotely through email.
